FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

Do you issue Electrical Certificates of Compliance (COC)?

Yes. We inspect and test your installation, fix anything that doesn't comply, and issue a valid COC. This is often needed when selling a property, for insurance, or after new electrical work.

Are your quotes really free?

Yes. Quotes are free and itemised. For fault-finding call-outs we will tell you the call-out fee before we come out, so you're never surprised.

Can you help with load shedding?

Absolutely. We design and install inverter, battery and solar systems sized to what you actually need to keep running, and we certify the installation.

Do you handle geyser insurance claims?

Yes. We can assess burst geysers, give your insurer the report and quote they need, and do the replacement, including the electrical COC for the geyser circuit.

Do you only do electrical work?

Electrical is our speciality, but we also do plumbing, CCTV and alarms, gate motors, electronics repairs, TV and network installs, and general maintenance.

Which areas do you service?

Contact us with your location and we'll confirm availability.
